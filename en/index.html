About me

I am a PhD student in Information and Communication Engineering at Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications. I undertook a joint doctoral research visit at Brunel University from 2024 to 2026. My research focuses on intelligent reflecting surface (IRS)-assisted wireless communication, particularly the interplay between beamforming, reflection phases, and receiver quantization in millimeter-wave MIMO systems.

In collaborative research, I study joint beamforming and IRS phase design using real-valued genetic algorithms, and how ADC dynamic range and resolution affect spectral efficiency and bit error rate. I have also contributed to communication-coverage modeling for mountainous environments, with experience in MATLAB-based system modeling, algorithm simulation, and performance analysis.
