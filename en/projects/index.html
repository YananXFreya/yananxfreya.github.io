Research

Research in propagation modeling, beamforming optimization, and receiver quantization, connecting algorithms with performance through system simulation.

01
Participation began in June 2023

TRIS-assisted communication coverage in mountainous environments

Natural Science Foundation of Chongqing project · Key participant

This project studies regional communication coverage in complex mountainous terrain. My work included reviewing RIS optimization methods, contributing to the project proposal, and modeling and simulating non-ideal propagation channels to support coverage-enhancement studies.

Work: literature and proposal preparation, propagation modeling, simulation, and performance analysis. Tools: MATLAB and Python.

02
2026 research output

Joint beamforming with modulo-ADC receivers

Collaborative research · ICC 2026

This collaborative work investigates the joint design of IRS phases, transceiver beamformers, and power allocation under blockage and quantization constraints. Algorithm simulations evaluate spectral efficiency, bit error rate, and convergence.

Output: an ICC 2026 conference paper. Methods and simulation figures are presented in the corresponding publication entry.

03
2025 research output

Hybrid beamforming for wideband multi-IRS systems

Collaborative research · EuCNC & 6G Summit 2025

This work studies multi-IRS-assisted MIMO-OFDM with a limited number of RF chains. A real-valued genetic algorithm jointly searches beamforming and reflection phases, with comparisons across IRS configurations and algorithmic baselines.

Output: poster P-2.23 at the 2025 EuCNC & 6G Summit.
