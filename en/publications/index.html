Joint Transceiver Beamforming and IRS Phase Shift Design for mmWave MIMO with Modulo ADCs

Yanan Xin, Wenyi Yan, Lu Gan, Qilie Liu, John Cosmas

ICC 2026 — IEEE International Conference on Communications

2026 · Glasgow, United Kingdom · pp. 1–6

DOI: 10.1109/ICC59461.2026.11587284

DOI / publication↗Repository & full text↗

The question

Millimeter-wave links are vulnerable to blockage, while a limited ADC dynamic range can introduce clipping distortion. This work models receiver quantization error in an IRS-assisted downlink MIMO system and studies spectral-efficiency maximization under transmit-power and constant-modulus constraints.

The approach

A real-valued genetic algorithm searches the IRS reflection phases. Transceiver beamformers are updated using singular value decomposition of the effective channel and water-filling power allocation. Simulations compare modulo ADCs with conventional ADCs operating at different dynamic ranges.

Key findings

Under the simulated conditions, modulo ADCs mitigate saturation-induced clipping and provide favorable spectral-efficiency and bit-error-rate performance at low resolution. The gains depend on dynamic range, bit depth, and system parameters, so comparisons should be read with their stated assumptions.

Real-Valued Genetic Algorithm-Based Hybrid Beamforming Optimization in Multi-IRSs-Aided mmWave MIMO-OFDM Systems

Yanan Xin, Qilie Liu, John Cosmas

2025 EuCNC & 6G Summit

2025 · Poznań, Poland · Poster Session II · P-2.23

Conference record↗View public poster↗

The question

Providing an independent RF chain for every antenna is costly in large-array systems. This work adopts a hybrid beamforming architecture and studies spectral-efficiency optimization in multi-IRS-assisted mmWave MIMO-OFDM systems.

The approach

A multi-IRS wideband channel model is combined with a real-valued genetic algorithm to jointly optimize transmitter hybrid beamforming and IRS phase shifts. Simulations compare different numbers of IRSs and optimization algorithms.

Key findings

For the studied layout and channel settings, optimized IRS-assisted configurations achieve higher spectral efficiency, with two IRSs outperforming one. RVGA also outperforms PSO and CCPSO across the tested SINR range.
